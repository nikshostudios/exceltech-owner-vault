# Grill me, one question at a time

Adapted from the Grill Me / Grilling interview method for a nontechnical business owner. This is a complete standalone skill; no separate plugin or slash command is required.

## Start

Read AGENTS.md, mi.md, Home.md, AIOS/onboarding.md and owner-onboard/SKILL.md in the sibling folder. Follow the private-working-copy check before saving business context. Resume existing answers rather than asking again.

Explain simply: “I will ask about one task at a time, then explain it back so you can correct me.” Start with a real working day, not an abstract ideal process.

## Keep an interview tree behind the scenes

Track confirmed answers, unresolved branches, dependencies and explicitly deferred questions in AIOS/onboarding.md. The owner never needs to manage this tree.

Choose the next unanswered question whose prerequisites are known. Ask ONE question, then wait. Do not dump the whole frontier or impose an arbitrary question limit. Follow up persistently when something is vague, but accept a pause or a skipped question without pressure.

Use concrete examples. Ask what happened last time, what the owner actually did and what made it difficult. Reflect contradictions kindly and ask which account is accurate. Suggest an answer only for a decision when useful; never suggest business facts the owner has not supplied.

For each important task establish its trigger, inputs, steps, people, tools, authoritative records, outputs, frequency, deadlines, exceptions and decisions. Discover tasks through answers rather than assuming the owner does every recruitment or administration function.

The agent investigates technical facts and current tool capabilities; it does not ask the owner questions it can answer by inspecting permitted files or official documentation. Ask the owner about their real preferences, access and business decisions.

## Confirm shared understanding

After a few answers, summarise the process in plain language and ask what is wrong or missing. Save only confirmed facts, with unknowns clearly marked. Keep the next question in the checkpoint so another agent can continue.

Use an early small task to test understanding and give the owner a useful result. Refine the process using their corrections. Do not require finishing all interviews before helping.

Do not implement a newly proposed process until the owner confirms the understanding. Existing setup and ordinary task assistance remain authorised. New external actions retain their own authorisation boundaries.

## Finish a branch

A branch is settled when the owner confirms your explanation, the desired outcome is clear and unresolved questions are answered or explicitly deferred. Then use owner-onboard to create the relevant context and, where useful, a small reusable skill. Preserve source records and avoid a competing business database.

The overall interview finishes when the important branches are settled or deliberately deferred and the owner confirms the summary. Never claim complete understanding merely because a questionnaire ran out. Save progress through vault-save and leave one clear next action.
