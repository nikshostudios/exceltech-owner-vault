# ExcelTech owner vault

A simple starting place for running your day with an AI assistant. Same core structure as the NST vault, with clean starter notes and no private business information.

## Start here

1. Click **Code → Download ZIP** on this page. Extract it into a private folder on your computer. Use the downloaded copy for your work, not this public repository.
2. In Obsidian, choose **Open folder as vault** and select that extracted folder.
3. In your AI assistant, open the same folder as a workspace. Nikhil can help with this one-time setup. A chat without access to the folder cannot update your vault automatically.
4. Copy the prompt in [START-HERE.md](START-HERE.md) into the assistant.

You do not need to learn the folders or remember commands. Start by saying what you need help with. The assistant will interview you one question at a time, help with one real task, and save confirmed context for next time.

## What you can say

- “Help me reply to this email.”
- “What should I focus on today?”
- “Turn these call notes into next actions.”
- “Help me simplify this recurring task.”
- “Save what we decided and tell me where to start tomorrow.”

## What is inside

| Folder | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Atlas | Your business, people and ways of working |
| Calendar | Daily notes, weekly reviews and quarterly priorities |
| Efforts | Active work and its next steps |
| Raw | Original source documents, preserved unchanged |
| Wiki | Summaries compiled from sources, with links back |
| AIOS | Instructions for the assistant and your personal workflows |
| Templates | Reusable note formats |

`mi.md` holds your working preferences. `Home.md` is your starting dashboard. `AGENTS.md` and `CLAUDE.md` tell the assistant how to help.

## Keep it simple

Begin with Obsidian, one AI assistant, and the tools you already use. Outlook and WhatsApp stay where they are. No connectors, new subscriptions or automations are needed to start. Add a tool only after a real task demonstrates the need.

## Public template, private working copy

Everything on GitHub is public. Never upload your completed vault, emails, CVs, phone numbers, contracts, passwords or financial records here. Downloading the ZIP gives you a separate working copy without a Git remote. The assistant checks this before saving business context. This repo distributes templates; it is not a company database or a backup of your working vault.

No productivity figure is guaranteed. Judge the setup by whether it saves you time on your own work.
